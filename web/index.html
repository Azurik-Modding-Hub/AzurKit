About AzurKit


AzurKit makes mods for Azurik: Rise of Perathia (Xbox): patch packs and sliders, randomized seeds and
characters to play as, built from your own copy of the game, USA or Europe, into a new image you play in xemu. It
never holds the game's disc image; so that it works before you pick yours, it ships the game's original config.xbr
(the Data tab's numbers) and pictures drawn from the game's files (the characters' views, the tracker's maps and
icons). It is free and open source.


AzurKit collects nothing: it adds no logging, analytics or tracking. Your image never leaves your
computer: AzurKit reads it and builds right here.
