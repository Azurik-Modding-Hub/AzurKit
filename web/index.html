About AzurKit


AzurKit makes mods for Azurik: Rise of Perathia (Xbox): patch packs and their settings, and randomized
seeds, built from your
own copy of the game, USA or Europe, into a new image you play in xemu. It holds no part of the game: everything it
changes is read from your image. It is free and open source, the rewrite of Azurik_Modkit.


AzurKit collects nothing: it adds no logging, analytics or tracking. Your image never leaves your
computer: AzurKit reads it and builds right here.
