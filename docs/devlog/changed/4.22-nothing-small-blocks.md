- **Nothing small blocks the workflow** (Step 4.22, D1170): every pytest run, the gate's, its flake stage's and CI's, reruns a failed test once at once (`tests/_rerun.py`, loaded by `tests/conftest.py`; failed fixtures are set up again). A test that passes its rerun is flaky: the run passes, the summary names it with its first try's error, `scripts/fullsuite.py` keeps it in the receipt's `flakes` and appends it to `flakes.jsonl` beside the receipts, and each CI job lists it in its summary (`AZURKIT_FLAKES`, `python -m tests._rerun`); a test that fails twice fails the run as before, and a rerun is recorded, never a skip. The perf gate's timings are advisory: an op over its budget or ceiling, past 1 s with no progress, or whose page froze in a gap (now the sample's `froze` mark, not an error) is a perf-debt row (the block's `debt`, printed and appended to `perf-debt.jsonl`; a Save's row carries the free disk), and the block fails only on an op whose median is over main's last passing full run's (`fullsuite.main_reference`, `perf.py --reference`) by more than 25 % and 0.5 s (`perf.REGRESSION`, `REGRESSION_S`), re-run three more times first, and on memory peaks, errors, native failures and a growing list as before; only such a candidate takes more samples. `scripts/land.py` drops `--release`: landing never waits for a release, a tag on a commit that is on main.
