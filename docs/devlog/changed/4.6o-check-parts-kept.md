- **A Check's parts are kept across visits** (Step 4.6o, D1167, the Check design's L2): each part of a variant's dress that Step 4.6n keeps in the process (its body's mesh, rig and paint, its size, its restyles, its LOD cuts and writes; and now the glTF clips a recipe reads and its kit's clips, whose in-process memos they replace) is handed to the host's store as `tools/characters/part_codec.py` keeps it (no pickle: its values and the dataclasses of the modules a part holds by their fields, runs in columns, zstd; a format, a class's fields or a class no part holds refused by name), under its key and the code's digest, and a part the process doesn't hold is taken from there before it is worked out. The app keeps them in its signed `swap` store, written behind the answer (`app.swapcache.Parts`); the site in IndexedDB's `azurkit-parts` (`web/parts.mjs`), which the page offers a Check by its recipe's content (its name, folder, labels and sounds left out) and keeps after its answer, the last 8 recipes' parts (the Tools tab's Storage lists it and Clear removes it). Booblord's own copy's parts keep in 28.8 MiB, and a copy checked under another name at a later launch took 6.3 s in the app's engine (60.1 s for the first copy), and at a later visit 18.6 s under the site's engine (Pyodide on Node, 141.3 s for the first; heap 1382 MiB at its answer), the same answer and dress byte for byte. `tests/test_variant_editor.py` holds a later visit's Check to a fresh one.
