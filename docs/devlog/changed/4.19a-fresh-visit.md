- **Each visit starts at the defaults unless Remember settings is ticked** (Step 4.19a; D1156, amending D915): a box in the bottom bar after Reset to defaults, off by default, its title "Keep your packs, settings, randomizer and character for your next visit". Ticked, the page keeps and restores its setup as D915 did (a link or a loaded recipe still takes its place); unticked, it keeps none and forgets the kept one at once. The choice is kept (the site's `azurkit.remember`, listed and cleared in the Tools tab's storage; the app's config `remember`, sent with each `api/setup` `{"remember", "setup"}`, `host.json` giving the setup only while it is true), and Reset to defaults leaves it as it is, naming a forgotten setup only when one was kept. A setup kept by 1.0.x, with no choice, is removed unread on the first visit (the site's load, the app's launch). The theme, the tracker's plan and marks, Content from, Show objects, installed content and the caches stay kept whatever the box says. The perf gate's A02 and A31/A32 tick the box at their first launch and A45's config holds `remember`, so each times the restore it timed.
