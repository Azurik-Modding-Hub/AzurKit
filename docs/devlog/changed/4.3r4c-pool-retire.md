- **A pool child gives its heap back** (Step 4.3r4c, round 4's capped-app lever): `app/procpool.py`'s child holds none of a part's inputs or results while it waits, and one whose peak resident size has reached `RETIRE` (256 MiB) sends its results and leaves, as only an exit returns that heap (a collection or the allocator's trim returned at most 2 MB a child); the next call takes a fresh child, about 0.3 s for 4. The peak is `getrusage`'s on macOS and `VmHWM` on Linux, whose `getrusage` starts a spawned child at its parent's peak. Windows has no peak to read and keeps its children: a named flaw. No byte changes: a call's results are each item's whichever child ran it.
