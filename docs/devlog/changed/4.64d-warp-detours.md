  ticked, each save platform it leaves from is a way out of its level and town's platform a way in for the detours
  (`difficulty.detours`), so a spot at a save platform is no longer rated off the path (at Standard, 529 spots off
  the path become 429). Easy, Hard and Expert seeds with the warp ticked are placed differently, so they are made at
  Seed difficulty's fill 3 (`SEED_DIFFICULTY_FILL`), and their links and plans made at fill 2 are refused by name;
  seeds without the warp keep fill 2 (`WARPLESS_FILL`, `settings.seed_difficulty_fill`), their plans as before and
  their links still open. Normal is untouched; no golden, `GENERATOR` or kernel change. Tests:
  `test_with_the_save_warp_counted_a_spot_beside_a_save_platform_is_no_detour`, and
  `test_seed_difficulty_rides_strings_and_files_and_v1_levels_load_at_the_nearest`'s new rows.
