- **A WAV whose header gives a rate under 1 kHz is refused by name before it is resampled** (Step 4.42a): the
  resample's length is the file's frames times 22,050 over its rate, so a handed WAV of a few bytes at 1 Hz took
  seconds and one of 10,000 frames hours and gigabytes, with no cancel point; the lowest real rate is 8 kHz.
