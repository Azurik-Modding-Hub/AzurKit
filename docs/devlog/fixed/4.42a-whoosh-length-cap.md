- **A recipe whose whooshes make more than 60 s in all is refused by name at load** (Step 4.42a): the whoosh makes
  each sample in turn, and a build makes every take of every `sfx` before it encodes one, so a handed recipe's
  `"length": 1000000` (any finite number was taken), or 23 sounds of 20 takes at 60 s each, hung a Check or a build
  for minutes to hours and filled memory; the limit is each whoosh's length times its takes, summed over the recipe,
  about ten times the game's longest inline wave and 6.8 times the most a shipped recipe makes (8.8 s).
