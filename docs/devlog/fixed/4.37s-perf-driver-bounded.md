- **The perf stage never waits on a page that stopped answering** (Step 4.37s, tests only): the perf driver's page calls had no bound of their own, and a WebKit that ended its pages' processes (its GPU process unresponsive three times in 30 s, `reason=GPUProcessCrashedTooManyTimes`) left S60's pass waiting on an evaluate 1 h 43 min, the suite lock held and seven gates behind it. Each evaluate, page opening and close, context and browser call and download path is now bounded through `_page.within`, so such a pass stops in seconds (WebKit's log names the ending) or at its bound, its ops' error naming the call, the ending, the run's WebKit processes and whether the browser still answers, and the run goes on.
