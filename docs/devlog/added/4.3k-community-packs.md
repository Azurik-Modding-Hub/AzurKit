- Community packs' engine (Step 4.3k, D988, D997): `tools/mods/community.py` checks a community pack (an Azurik-Custom-Content `pack` entry's `packs/<id>/pack.json`: a release-free mods file of AzurKit's own packs and `gamedata/*.xbr` edits, self-contained), gives the content folder's Install check of a pack entry (`refusal`, for `content.verify`'s hook), resolves a recipe's `community` pins (installed, untouched since its install, at its pack.json's sha12, else named with what to Install or Update), merges its built-in packs with the recipe's own (a pack set two ways refused by name, equal copies one) and plans its edits as their own `community <id>` layer; `edits.preset.from_manifest` carries a build's community layers back as the mods file's `community` layer (id and sha12). Not wired into the sdk or the hosts yet; no pack ships.
