- The Data tab's engine (Step 4.7a, D986): `azurkit.tools.data` reads config.xbr's 18 keyed tables from the vanilla file it ships (both releases' file, byte for byte) and gives the tab's index (the tables by Modkit's categories; the critter, pickup, armor and staff pickers with their info rows), the Player, Critter, Item, Armor and Weapon views of Modkit's Game Data editor (all 201 match Modkit's, cell for cell, and so do its pickers), a table as a grid, and `check`, which turns a cell's edit into the recipe's `xbr` `set` operation or says why the cell can't hold it, with its type and an example; each cell carries its note, unit, range hint and editor from `schema.json` (Modkit's 110 checked entries, by config.xbr's own names). A recipe's `set` takes `col` (a column of an entity named twice) and `fill` (an empty cell), and refuses a number an int32 cell can't hold once the game rounds or truncates it (D45).
