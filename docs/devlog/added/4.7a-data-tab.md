- The Data tab's engine (Step 4.7a, D986): `azurkit.tools.data` reads config.xbr's 18 keyed tables from the vanilla file it ships (both releases' file, byte for byte) and gives the tab's index (the tables by Modkit's categories; the critter, pickup, armor and staff pickers with their info rows), the Player, Critter, Item, Armor and Weapon views of Modkit's Game Data editor (all 201 match Modkit's, cell for cell, and so do its pickers), a table as a grid, and `check`, which turns a cell's edit into the recipe's `xbr` `set` operation or says why the cell can't hold it, with its type and an example; each cell carries its note, unit, range hint and editor from `schema.json` (Modkit's 110 checked entries, by config.xbr's own names, and the 22 damage records of critters_damage it left out). A recipe's `set` takes `col` (a column of an entity named twice) and `fill` (an empty cell), and refuses a number an int32 cell can't hold once the game rounds or truncates it (D45), critters_damage's cell for each of the 57 damage records among them.
- The Data tab's page (Step 4.7a): `web/data.mjs` draws the six views from the engine's answers (sections of mini-tables; All tables a grid, an entity a row, sorted by a column's header, Changed only), edits a cell on a click, Enter or F2 (a dropdown or a checkbox where the cell's value set says), shows a refusal as one line under the cell, marks a changed cell ● and a cell a ticked pack writes ⚠, and keeps the edits with Revert, Reset table, Undo, Redo and Clear edits; `web/picker.mjs` is the shared gallery picker (a search box over grouped entries, a picture where one ships). Both wait for the hub's wiring (sdk.data and data_edit, the panel, the worker's and the app's routes).
